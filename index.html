Starfall Siege Survivor

Privacy Policy
Effective November 8, 2026

In short

  • The game works offline and has no account or sign-up.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We never receive it.
  • We don’t collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. It only adds online leaderboards.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information. You can limit it in your Android settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Starfall Siege Survivor (“the game”) is an Android game made by AstroCrafts Digital Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, who handles it, and the choices you have.

You can reach us at starfallsiege@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: credits, upgrades, unlocked weapons and heroes, settings, your best scores and times, and your list of top runs. It also keeps your best score and time that haven’t been uploaded to a leaderboard yet.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. If you use Android’s device backup, Android may include the game’s data in your backups, under Google’s terms.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, or collect, your name, email address, home address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone or camera data. We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not create accounts.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game uploads your best score and your longest survival time to Google Play Games Services so they can appear on online leaderboards.

When you sign in, Google shares your Play Games profile (your player ID, gamer name and avatar) with the game. Other players can see your gamer name, score and time on the leaderboard. This information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address.

If you don’t sign in, the game works fully and your scores stay on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some ads are optional, such as watching a video to earn extra credits. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares:

  • your device’s IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with the game and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information
  • device identifiers, including your Android advertising ID

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choices at any time:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads).
  • Turn off personalized ads in your Google ad settings.

To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information. We only see summary reports, such as ad revenue.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The game may offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads or buying credits. Google Play processes these purchases. We never see your card number or other payment details. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, such as which item was bought and an order identifier, so the game can give you the item.

Crash and performance reports

If you have chosen to share diagnostic data with Google, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports about the game. We use them only to find and fix problems.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to run the game, show leaderboards and ads, process purchases and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone other than the Google services described above, except where the law requires it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Retention and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then Starfall Siege Survivor, then Storage, and clear the game’s data.
  • Leaderboard data: this is held by Google, not by us. You can delete your Play Games data through your Play Games profile or your Google Account.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

We do not store personal information on servers of our own, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you need help with any of this, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to certain uses, or to withdraw consent. Because the information described in this policy is held by Google rather than by us, you can use these rights through your Google Account and the settings described above. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

Security

The Google services used by the game send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when we add features. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Continuing to use the game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
